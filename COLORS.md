## Usage Summary

### Most Frequently Used Colors
1. **#1e40af** (Blue-800) - Primary brand color, buttons, links
2. **#6b7280** (Gray-500) - Body text, secondary text
3. **#ffffff** (White) - Backgrounds, text on dark
4. **#059669** (Green-600) - Success states, seller actions
5. **#f3f4f6** (Gray-100) - Light backgrounds
6. **#9ca3af** (Gray-400) - Muted text, footers
7. **#3b82f6** (Blue-500) - Secondary links, accents

### Color Usage by Context
- **Primary Actions**: #1e40af (Blue-800)
- **Success Actions**: #059669 (Green-600)
- **Destructive Actions**: #dc2626 (Red-600)
- **Backgrounds**: #ffffff (White), #f3f4f6 (Gray-100), #111827 (Gray-900)
- **Text**: #111827 (Gray-900), #6b7280 (Gray-500), #9ca3af (Gray-400)
- **Borders**: #e5e7eb (Gray-200), #d1d5db (Gray-300)
